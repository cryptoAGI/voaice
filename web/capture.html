vCLONE — capture and measure

A microphone, eight metrics, and a voiceprint over them. Nothing leaves this page.

press RECORD — the browser will ask for the microphone

What this does, and what it does not

It measures. It takes audio from your microphone, computes the eight acoustic metrics the DeltaVerse oscilloscope defines, encodes each as an 18-decimal fixed-point integer, and hashes the canonical form into a vprint — SHA-256, SHA-512, and the digest read as a uint256. You can keep the recording as a WAV and the identity as a .voaice.

It does not clone. There is no synthesiser here and nothing in this repository will turn a capture into a speaking voice. The synthesis field in a .voaice is the seam where one would attach, and it is null in every file shipped here.

A vprint is a fingerprint of a measurement, not of a person. Change the FFT size, the sample rate, the window, the microphone or the length of the excerpt and every metric moves, so the print moves with it. Two recordings of the same person measured differently produce different prints. It is evidence that two files came from the same measured signal. It is not a biometric and must not be used as one — the comparison tool refuses to compare prints whose measurement parameters differ rather than returning a number that looks like an answer.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no server behind this page. The audio never leaves the tab, the WAV is encoded in the browser, and the .voaice is written from what you can see on screen. Close the tab and it is gone.
